Privacy policy

Max Drive is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It collects nothing from anyone else, because there is no one else.

It doesn't sell anything, show ads, or pass data to anyone the owner hasn't chosen to use. Its use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke its access at any time in their Google account settings. Thanks for stopping by, though.
